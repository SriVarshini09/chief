What Chief does

📅 Calendar

Spots a crowded day, a clash or a meeting with no room to breathe, and suggests a fix.

✉️ Inbox

Reads Gmail for what needs you, and drafts replies you can read before anything is sent.

⏰ Reminders

A morning brief and gentle check-ins, on the web page or on Telegram.

📍 Places and directions

Finds what's near you and how to get there, using your location only if you say yes.

🍴 Booking prep

Gets a table or a trip ready with the right links and details. You do the booking.

✅ Asks first

Nothing is sent, booked, moved or paid until you tap Approve. Recent changes can be undone.

Getting set up takes a minute

  1. Create your account: your name, email and a password.
  2. Connect Google so Chief can read Gmail and Calendar.
  3. Optional: connect Telegram for the morning brief, and share your location for places nearby.
